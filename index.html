About AYUTH

What is AYUTH?

AYUTH (IP-SAKTI Sahayak) is an AI-powered assistant that provides guidance on intellectual property (IP) and regulatory matters specific to Ayurvedic inventions and innovations.

Scope

  • Patent eligibility and inventive step
  • Traditional Knowledge (TK) and TKDL overlaps
  • Biological resource rules and biopiracy prevention
  • Trademarks vs. Patents
  • Geographical Indications (GI)
  • AYUSH manufacturing licenses
  • International filing routes

NOT Covered

Medical advice, treatment claims, clinical efficacy, or therapeutic guidance. For health-related questions, consult a qualified healthcare provider.

Important Disclaimer

This tool provides informational guidance only and is NOT a substitute for professional legal advice from a registered patent attorney or IP counselor. Always consult qualified professionals before filing patents or making regulatory submissions.
